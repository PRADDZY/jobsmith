# JobSmith neatHack demo narration

1. **The decision** — JobSmith answers one narrow question: is this support role ready for a supervised AI pilot? A perfect score alone is not approval.
2. **Role and task** — We start with a live respond.io support listing. JobSmith extracts one duty, maps its workflow, and runs synthetic cases against relevant policy.
3. **Failure and recovery** — The first attempt hit a provider-format issue and exceeded a response bound. The run stopped safely, kept its workflow state, and resumed after the fix.
4. **Passing evidence** — The later run passed two of two practice cases and two of two held-out checks, with zero unsupported policy claims. It recommends only a supervised pilot candidate.
5. **Limitations** — Across these small synthetic runs, the combined score moved from two of four to four of four. The selected duties differed, so this is directional evidence, not a controlled comparison.
6. **Partner roles** — Neatlogs instruments each pilot case for runtime review. Entire's code graph links evaluation to policy checks and bounded verification. cfo.ai stores an editable illustrative operating plan, not observed business results.
7. **Human boundary** — JobSmith doesn't make hiring decisions or act on customers. People review risk and own the pilot gate. JobSmith helps a team decide what to test next, with evidence attached.
